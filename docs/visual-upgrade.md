# Canvas and early-years visual update

The existing Canvas renderer now rotates its cached spherical lighting over 8 seconds, adds a dark radial rim, and uses a radial green background. Arrowheads use four world-space cone ribs before perspective projection. Physics force/velocity/acceleration vectors use orange/cyan/gold; corresponding scene legends are updated. The Class 12 Coulomb scene draws dashed separation and numerically integrated field lines for its two fixed positive charges. Field paths stop at the symmetry null; this scene does not simulate negative charges. Arrow lengths and object sizes are schematic, with force lengths proportional within each scene's chosen scale.

Every science scene now has a non-interactive HTML HUD with up to 2 measurements/controls and current animation speed. Parameter/time/energy plots use a 160×100 inset at the bottom left. Full measurements and graph descriptions remain outside the canvas for accessibility. Threshold announcements cover heating/melting, signed slider zero crossings and the Class 12 RLC resonance. Announcements last 1.5 seconds; reduced-motion avoids the fade. Animation time, highlights, dashed lines and time-graph sampling stop together when paused or hidden.

Early-years enhancements are confined to `early/index.html` and its styles; existing lesson/progress code remains intact. Bright cream/pastel backgrounds, large letters, bouncing pictures, keyboard/click pop, subject colours and sidebar icons/stars are added. A 6-circle, 1-second confetti canvas marks the first exploration of 3 cards in the current lesson/session. Local speech utterance start/end/error events control audio button glow when a local voice exists. Low-power/reduced-motion disables decorative motion. The main hub's LKG/UKG cards now have bright colours, decorative emoji and prominent button-style links.

Verification: 760 automated tests pass, including 3D cone geometry and equal-charge field symmetry/extrema checks. Chrome checked the charge HUD/inset, speed, three-card celebration, letter sizing, keyboard pop, boiling threshold and graph pause. Responsive early/science frames at 360/768/1366 had no document overflow and no Devanagari digits. Real Android/Edge/Firefox, installed-PWA offline execution and sustained performance/memory profiling remain unverified. Actual speech playback needs an installed local voice and was unavailable in this browser; fallback remains available.
