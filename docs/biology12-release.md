# Class 12 Biology / कक्षा 12 जीव विज्ञान

Integrated into the existing Vigyan Darshan science reader and subject hub. Class 12 now has Physics, Chemistry and Biology; the existing Maths book remains available in Ganit Darshan. Student storage stays under `vigyan-darshan-v3` with the existing class/subject bookmark scheme and new `biology12-01`…`biology12-13` chapter identities. Older progress is retained.

## Content

13 chapters, 39 interactive scenes, 104 detailed note sections, 65 worked examples, 130 MCQs with 520 option reasons, and 78 written questions. Each chapter has at least 1200 Hindi note words, English explanations, section questions and answers, five revision lines and a concept map. No lesson-plan, homework or key-term section added. English titles precede Hindi titles; numerals are English throughout.

The sequence covers flowering-plant reproduction, human reproduction, reproductive health, inheritance, molecular genetics, evolution, health and disease, useful microbes, biotechnology principles and applications, populations, ecosystems, and biodiversity conservation. Notes and questions are original explanations aligned with the 13-chapter NCERT sequence and prescribed UP Board 2026–27 scope.

## Visual models

Local Canvas schematics illustrate embryo-sac development, double fertilisation, gametogenesis, reproductive stages, clinical-education mechanisms, Punnett squares, ABO codominance, antiparallel DNA, information flow, the lac operon, evolutionary evidence, immune memory, pathogen groups, microbial processes, recombinant DNA, gel separation, insulin, Bt and RNA interference. Quantitative models cover Hardy–Weinberg frequencies, ideal PCR doubling, growth, quadrat density, energy transfer, GPP/NPP and a species–area fit.

Every scene has a free control, two missions, range/stage-specific live text, Why, a real-life connection, linked notes and interaction-gated Quick Check. Selected stages are snapshots; animation pulses are symbolic. Explicit notes distinguish ideal probabilities, fitted values and illustrative transfer efficiencies from measured observations. No external runtime dependency or CDN is added.
