## Chapter 13 scientific conventions / वैज्ञानिक परंपराएँ

- Use the ecological 10% transfer rule as an approximate classroom estimate. The NCERT wording describes an average, so the fixed numerical efficiency in a scene must be labelled a model rather than a universal measured constant.
- Distinguish one-way energy flow from nutrient cycling. Energy is dispersed, mainly as heat; decomposers return nutrients, not sunlight.
- Biomagnification concerns increasing **concentration of the same appropriate persistent chemical** across trophic levels. It does not mean that energy increases upward. Do not assign a universal multiplier or present illustrative index values as real DDT measurements.
- The biodegradation slider uses **model weeks**, not universal lifetimes for leaves or plastics. Material, microorganisms and environmental conditions affect decomposition. Fragmentation alone is not complete biological decomposition.
- Differentiate protective upper-atmosphere ozone from potentially harmful ozone near the ground; differentiate ozone depletion from global warming. No claim about today's ozone-hole size or current recovery date is needed for this chapter.
- All expanded notes, examples and assessment items are original instructional writing. The site does not reproduce the textbook's full chapter prose, exercise set or illustrations.
