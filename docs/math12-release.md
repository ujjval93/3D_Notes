# Class 12 Mathematics / कक्षा 12 गणित

Added to the existing Ganit Darshan reader and Vigyan Darshan subject hub, with the existing class navigation, five learning tabs and local progress identities. The Maths key stays `ganit-darshan-v1`; Science retains `vigyan-darshan-v3`. Class-prefixed quiz, completion, mistake and bookmark keys preserve older classes.

## Content

13 chapters, 39 interactive scenes, 104 detailed bilingual note sections, 65 worked examples, 130 MCQs with 520 option reasons, and 78 written questions. Every chapter has at least 1200 Hindi note words, section questions and answers, five revision points and a concept map. No lesson-plan, homework or key-term section was added. All headings place English first and Hindi second; all numerals are English.

## Curriculum

Original explanations and exercises follow NCERT Class XII Mathematics Parts I and II and UPMSP Mathematics 2026–27. The prescribed UP Board scope includes function composition and inverse-function/trigonometric properties. Planes, random variables and binomial-distribution units are not added as prescribed content.

- https://upmsp.edu.in/Downloads/Syllabus/Class12/131-Maths-Class-12.pdf
- https://ncert.nic.in/textbook.php?lemh1=0-6
- https://ncert.nic.in/textbook.php?lemh2=0-7

## Visual lessons

Three local SVG scenes per chapter cover equivalence classes and mappings, principal inverse functions, matrix operations, determinant area and singularity, continuity and tangent slopes, optimisation and related rates, Riemann rectangles and signed integrals, enclosed areas, differential-equation solution families, vector products, spatial lines and shortest distances, feasible regions and corner points, conditional probability and Bayes trees. Free sliders, two missions, live calculations, Why and interaction-gated Quick Check accompany every scene. Inverse-function and derivative points support dragging and keyboard arrows. Spatial views explicitly use an oblique projection. No external libraries or CDN assets are added.
